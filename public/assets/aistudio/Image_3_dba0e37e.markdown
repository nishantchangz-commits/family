## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, communal vitality, and bustling organization of a modern Indian culinary home. It merges the warmth of traditional home cooking with the precision of contemporary quick-commerce inventory logistics. The visual tone is hospitable, tactile, and effortlessly modernâ€”avoiding rustic clutter in favor of crisp editorial structure, organic softness, and appetizing vibrancy.

The design movement combines **Modern Warm Minimal** with **Tactile Utility**. High-clarity micro-surfaces, pill-shaped tags, and rounded cards rest gently upon soft cream canvasses. Interactions feel physical and responsiveâ€”mimicking pantry organization jars, tactile meal-voting chips, and dynamic visual indicators for pantry restocking.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the sensory tones of Indian spice pantries and fresh herb markets:

- **Primary (`#D35400` / Deep Saffron Amber):** The core active accent. Used for primary CTAs, meal confirmation buttons, key active states, and focal dish highlights. Evokes roasted spices, tadka, and hospitality.
- **Secondary (`#059669` / Fresh Mint Basil Green):** Serves as the abundance and inventory affirmative color. Represents freshly stocked items, optimal pantry levels, dietary vegetarian identifiers, and completed grocery runs.
- **Tertiary (`#EAB308` / Express Turmeric Yellow):** Dedicated to quick-commerce delivery hooks, urgent low-stock warnings, and rapid re-order integrations. Provides clear contrast against deep slate text.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` / Rich Slate Navy):** Provides high-contrast, modern legibility across text hierarchy, structural borders, and icons, replacing harsh pure black with warm, ink-like depth.
- **Canvas & Containers (`#FDFBF7` / Warm Cream):** Canvas surfaces use an unbleached, buttery cream tone to soften eye strain and impart comforting culinary warmth, paired with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces to layer elevation cleanly.

## Typography

The type pairing unites the welcoming geometric friendliness of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and interactive tags with the neutral, hyper-legible utility of **Inter** for dense pantry tables, ingredient quantities, and nutritional meta-labels.

- Use **Plus Jakarta Sans** in bold and semibold weights for recipe titles, member greetings, inventory metric displays, and button text to project optimistic domestic warmth.
- Use **Inter** exclusively for ingredient volumes, instructions, timestamp tracking, and dense tabular values where neutral, uniform character tracking prevents visual clutter.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a responsive fluid grid system designed for kitchen scenarios: handheld smartphone checks during meal prep, tablet stands on kitchen counters, and desktop planning screens.

- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Tap targets are calibrated to at least 48px to accommodate one-handed usage while cooking.
- **Tablet (768px â€“ 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Split-screen view supports simultaneous meal planning alongside current ingredient inventories.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with `3rem` margins and `2rem` gutters, capped at a maximum width of `1280px` to maintain focused culinary lists without overstretched tracking lines.

Internal card components observe strict rhythm steps: `space-xs` (4px) for tight pill metadata, `space-sm` (8px) between icon and text labels, `space-md` (16px) for interior padding of pantry items, and `space-lg` (24px) for container padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm, tinted ambient depth rather than clinical greyscale drop-shadows. The base canvas (`#FDFBF7`) supports pure-white cards with warm, low-contrast diffuse shadows tinted with terracotta and spice undertones:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FDFBF7` background.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(226, 109, 30, 0.08)`, ambient shadow `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04), 0 4px 16px -4px rgba(211, 84, 0, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Active Cards):** Ambient shadow `0 8px 24px -6px rgba(211, 84, 0, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Grocery Drawers, Floating Restock Trays):** Shadow `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.14), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(211, 84, 0, 0.08)`.

Quick-commerce instant delivery callouts use a hairline gradient ring composed of tertiary yellow (`#FACC15`) and primary saffron (`#D35400`) to signal real-time responsiveness.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, comfortable shapes reflecting kitchen warmth and family safety:

- **Containers & Cards:** Utilize `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) to produce soft edges that feel tactile and approachable.
- **Modal Dialogs & Elevated Drawers:** Utilize `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) along top edges for mobile action sheets.
- **Interactive Pills, Tags, and Status Indicators:** Utilize full circular pill styling (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for ingredient status flags, category filters, and vote counters.
- **Gauges & Meter Bars:** Encapsulated in full-radius pill tracks with smooth inner fills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Cook, Order, Confirm):** Saffron Amber background (`#D35400`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.75rem` vertical and `1.5rem` horizontal padding, `0.5rem` border radius (`rounded-md`). Subtle micro-interaction spring bounce on tap.
- **Secondary (Add Ingredient, Suggest Alternate):** Warm cream background with a 1.5px border of slate-navy at 15% opacity (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.15)`), slate-navy text (`#1E293B`).
- **Quick-Commerce Express Action:** High-saturation Turmeric Yellow (`#FACC15`), rich navy text (`#1E293B`), bold weight, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), accompanied by a lightning or delivery icon.

### Chips & Tags
- **Pantry Availability Status:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`). "In Stock" uses Mint Basil background (`#ECFDF5`) with bold green text (`#059669`). "Low Stock" uses warm yellow background (`#FEF9C3`) with amber text (`#854D0E`). "Out of Stock" uses warm rose-peach tint (`#FFF1F2`) with red-slate text (`#BE123C`).
- **Meal Vote Chips:** Tactile toggle buttons with family member avatars nested inside. Active state fills with deep Saffron Amber (`#D35400`); idle state remains transparent with soft slate border.
